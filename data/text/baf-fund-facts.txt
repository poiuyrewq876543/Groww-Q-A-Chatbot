Investment Objective
To provide long term capital appreciation / income from a dynamic mix of equity and debt investments. There is no assurance that
the investment objective of the Scheme will be achieved.


Investment Strategy
• The fund dynamically manages its investment into two broad divisions –in equity and equity related instruments (65-100%), and
the balance in debt instruments. The fund endeavors to give twin advantage, the growth potential of equity as well as strong
foundation of debt
• Current asset allocation is 72% total equity and 28% allocation in debt and money market instruments as on September 30, 2026.
• The investment framework is based on valuations, assessment of macro scenario and bottom-up assessment of investment
opportunities.
